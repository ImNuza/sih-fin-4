## Five years from MAS regulatory returns (2021 to 2025)

Every insurer files a yearly return with MAS on the same form, and MAS publishes it free ([Income, code I585C](https://www.mas.gov.sg/statistics/insurance-statistics/insurance-company-returns/i585c), and for years before Sep 2022 the [co-operative, code I808C](https://www.mas.gov.sg/statistics/insurance-statistics/insurance-company-returns/i808c)). Because the form is the same every year, this is the only 5-year series that can be compared year to year.

S$ million unless stated. Whole company, all lines of business:

| Year | Gross premiums | Net income after tax | Total assets | General insurance gross premiums | Profit after tax in Income's own reports (IFRS) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2021 | 4,617.1 | 421.9 | S$46.8bn | 373.5 | not checked |
| 2022 | 4,316.0 | **−690.4** | S$42.1bn | 446.4 | not checked |
| 2023 | 3,747.6 | 95.1 | S$43.1bn | 492.1 | not published for 2023 alone (18-month report) |
| 2024 | 3,362.9 | 65.4 | S$44.1bn | 524.6 | 44.8 |
| 2025 | 3,445.2 | 205.5 | S$45.3bn | 529.4 | 118.9 |

Where each number comes from:
- Gross premiums and net income: Form A2, rows 1 and 25.
- Total assets: Form A1, row 27.
- General insurance gross premiums: Form G1, row 6, Singapore Insurance Fund.
- The co-operative's returns have no total column, so its totals are the sum of all fund columns.

**Why 2022 is a loss:** the co-operative's 2022 return shows a net investment loss of S$4.0 billion. The insurance business moved from the co-operative to Income Insurance Limited on 1 Sep 2022.
- The 2022 row is the co-operative's return, which is dated 1 Jan to 31 Dec 2022. Total assets in that row are Income Insurance Limited's at 31 Dec 2022, because the co-operative's 2022 return has no balance sheet.
- Income Insurance Limited also filed a 1 Sep to 31 Dec 2022 return: gross premiums S$1,156.6m, net loss S$128.8m. The two 2022 returns may overlap, so **don't add them together**.

**Regulatory and IFRS profit are different measures.** The MAS return is on the regulatory basis. Income's own reports use IFRS 17 from 2024. For 2025, the MAS return shows S$205.5m and the annual report S$118.9m. Quote each with its own source.

General insurance, Singapore Insurance Fund (Form G1): the claims and combined ratios by year are in Notion, "Loss ratio target from MAS insurer filings". They match this extraction: 2025 earned premium S$485.9m, claims ratio 55.6%, combined ratio 91.6%.

## Why it matters for the pitch

- **Underwriting in general insurance is getting worse.** The combined ratio for general insurance and group business went from 87% to 96% in two years, which analysts put down to claims and motor price competition. That is above Income's 2028 target of below 95%. A new general insurance product must not push it higher.
- **Our tariff's built-in margin:** in `api/default_tariff.json`, the premium before GST is expected claims × 1.25 ÷ 0.75:
  - 1.25 adds the 15% uncertainty load and the 10% catastrophe load
  - dividing by 0.75 covers the 25% expense ratio

  So expected claims are 60% of premium. Adding 25% expenses gives an expected combined ratio of **85%**, below the 95% target. The loads are what absorb a year worse than history.
- **Expense ratio is above the brief:** the brief says expenses should be "within about 20% of premium". At 20%, expected claims would be 64% of premium and the expected combined ratio 84%.
