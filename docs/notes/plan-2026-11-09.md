## Key findings (for the pitch)

- Heat: WBGT 32°C or more on 22% of days at one station, but usually under 1 hour. Too frequent for a simple trigger.
- Rain (10 years): an hour of 20 mm or more on 5.1% of days at one gauge. Local: 13% of gauges hit on an event day. Peaks in November and April.
- Haze: 0 to 6 unhealthy days a year in 2016 to 2025. 39 in 2026, including 23 days in a row (3 to 25 Oct 2026, peak PSI 230). Island-wide: 70% of regions hit on an event day.

## Gotchas for the next agent

- **data.gov.sg rate limit:** heavy use gives HTTP 429 for minutes. `scripts/dgs.py` switches between the production and development keys, spaces calls and uses 2 workers. Run one fetch at a time. Python's default User-Agent gets HTTP 403. `dgs.py` sends its own.
- Readings can be the string `"NA"`. Parse to null.
- The WBGT station network grew from 8 to 30. Trends over time use the 7 core stations (95%+ of days).
- Station S135 was renamed. Group by station ID, not name.
- Streamlit caches data. After a data change, restart the app.
