How you get paid

Three steps, and none of them is yours.

  1. 1 NEA publishes the readings Haze, rain, heat and PUB flood alerts near your stall. Public data, every hour.
  3. 2 Payung checks your trigger PSI above 100, 20 mm of rain in a work hour, 2 hours of extreme heat, or a flood alert.
  5. 3 PayNow pays you Usually the same day, with an SMS. No claim to make. S$195 paid
