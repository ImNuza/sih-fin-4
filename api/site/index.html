How the payout is made

The premium comes from the same four parts insurers like Swiss Re and Munich Re use to price catastrophe risk: where the hazard strikes, who is exposed, how much it hurts them, and the money terms on top. Open the Method and data panel on the risk atlas for every step.

Open the risk atlas
  1. NEA publishes the reading. The 24-hour PSI, the rain at the 3 gauges nearest you (the middle reading of those that reported), the hours of dangerous heat, or a PUB flood alert.
  2. The reading crosses a trigger level. The levels are fixed and public:
    • PSI above 100
    • 20 mm of rain in a work hour
    • 2 hours at WBGT 32 °C
    • an active flood alert
  3. The day gets a loss score. How much of a normal day's sales that reading costs your kind of business, from 0% to 100%.
  4. You get paid. Your tier's daily amount times the loss score, usually the same day. No claim form, no assessor.

Your price depends on your own area's weather history.
